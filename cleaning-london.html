Why the move-out clean decides your deposit

Your deposit is judged against the check-in inventory report. Cleaning is where tenancies lose money — not damage, not rent arrears.

  • Cleaning is the top reason for deposit claims — 29.38% of all claims in 2025, the most common cause five years in a row (Deposit Protection Service).
  • 63% of UK renters have had money deducted for cleaning, in a pool of deductions worth over £724 million a year (SpareRoom).
  • London tenants search for cleaners more than anyone — 115 cleaner searches per 100,000 people, the highest rate in the UK (Capital on Tap 2026 UK Trade Report).
  • The oven, limescale and carpets are the items inventory clerks photograph first — and the ones a standard domestic clean usually misses.
Know your rights — Tenant Fees Act 2019 Your landlord or agent cannot legally require you to hire a professional cleaner. What they can do is deduct for cleaning needed to return the property to its check-in standard, beyond fair wear and tear. A professional clean with a re-clean guarantee is how you take that argument off the table — it is protection, not an obligation.

End of tenancy cleaning — common questions

        

        

          

            How much does end of tenancy cleaning cost in London in 2026?
            
London move-out cleans typically run £220–£500+ by property size: from about £185 for a studio to £495–£620 for a five-bedroom house. Across the UK, a typical end of tenancy clean costs around £260, with the middle half of customers paying £207–£327 including extras. Carpet steam cleaning (£45 per room) is the add-on that moves the price most.

          

          

            Can my landlord force me to book a professional clean?
            
No. Under the Tenant Fees Act 2019, a landlord or letting agent in England cannot require a professional clean as a condition of your tenancy. They can deduct from your deposit for cleaning needed to restore the property to its check-in condition (beyond fair wear and tear) — which is why cleaning is the single most common deduction, at 29.38% of deposit claims in 2025 (Deposit Protection Service).

          

          

            What if the letting agent isn't happy with the clean?
            
Every firm quoting through this service offers a free re-clean within 72 hours. Send the agent's or inventory clerk's comments within that window and the firm returns to re-clean the flagged items at no charge. Book your clean at least 2–3 days before the check-out inspection so the window is usable.

          

          

            How many firms will contact me?
            
A maximum of two. Large marketplaces sell the same enquiry to 5–10 pros; here the cap is 2, your details are shared only with your explicit consent, and firms are told to respond fast — most customers book whoever replies first and properly.

          

          

            When should I book the clean?
            
After the property is fully emptied, and ideally 2–3 days before your check-out inspection. That leaves room to use the free re-clean if the clerk flags anything. End-of-month dates in East London fill fastest — E14 alone is the busiest move-out postcode in the capital.

          

          

            Is the oven really included?
            
Yes — a full oven deep clean (racks, trays, door glass and seals) is in every base quote here, along with inside windows, descaling and the fridge/freezer. The oven is the single most photographed item at check-out; a "clean" that skips it is the most common reason move-out cleans get rejected.

          

          

            Do I pay this website anything?
            
No. The service is free for tenants, landlords and agents. Firms pay a flat £20 for each verified request they receive — that's the whole business model, and it's why requests are capped at 2 buyers instead of being sold to everyone.

          

          

            What is included in an end of tenancy deep clean checklist?
            
A full checklist covers every room: inside ovens, fridges and cupboards, descaling bathrooms, washing windows inside, skirting boards, light switches, and floors throughout. Professional cleaners work to an inventory clerk's standard, since the clean is judged against the property's check-in report.

          

          

            Can I dispute a deposit deduction for cleaning?
            
Yes. If your landlord deducts cleaning costs from your deposit, ask for the check-in and check-out inventory reports plus cleaning invoices as evidence. You can raise a dispute free with your deposit protection scheme (DPS, MyDeposits or TDS), which will judge whether the deduction is fair — a professional clean with a receipt is your strongest defence.

          

          

            How long does an end of tenancy clean take?
            
As a guide, with a small team working to the inventory checklist: studio — 2–3 hours, 1-bed — 3–4 hours, 2-bed — 4–5 hours, 3-bed — 5–7 hours. Heavy limescale, a neglected oven or a furnished property add time, which is why quotes here are fixed by property size rather than by the hour — the price you agree is the price you pay even if the clean runs long.

          

          

            Are the oven and fridge/freezer included, or charged as extras?
            
Both are included in the standard clean on every quote here — a full oven deep clean and a defrosted, sanitised fridge/freezer are part of the base price, not add-ons. What is charged extra is listed openly in the form before you submit: carpet steam cleaning (£45 per room), upholstery, exterior windows, balcony wash and wall-mark cleaning. If a firm tries to add the oven on the day, that is not how quotes from this service work.

          

          

            What if a cleaning deduction is still raised after a professional clean?
            
Two protections kick in. First, if the agent or inventory clerk flags specific checklist items, the firm's 72-hour free re-clean covers them — send the clerk's comments straight to the firm. Second, your clean leaves an evidence trail: keep the firm's invoice and receipt, your quote request reference, and any before-and-after photos, and ask for the check-out report — together these show the property was professionally cleaned to the checklist standard. That evidence is what a deposit scheme weighs if a deduction is still proposed. We can't promise a particular outcome — the scheme decides — but a documented professional clean is a far stronger position than no paperwork at all.

          

          

            Do the cleaners bring their own supplies and equipment?
            
Yes. Professional end of tenancy cleaners arrive with their own cleaning products, tools and equipment — including the specialist products needed for oven degreasing, limescale removal and carpet steam cleaning. You don't need to stock up on anything or leave supplies behind. All you need to provide is an emptied property with working water and electricity, plus access on the day — keys, parking details and entry codes are worth adding to the notes field on the quote form so the team can get straight to work.

          

          

            What should I do with pets during the clean?
            
Pets are best kept out of the property while the clean is running — doors are opened constantly, strong cleaning products are in use, and a team moving room to room with equipment is stressful for animals. The simplest option is to have your pet stay with a friend, family member or sitter for the day. If that's not possible, keep them in one closed room that the team cleans last, and mention it in the notes field on the quote form so the firm can plan around it. Note that pets should be fully moved out with you by check-out — inventory clerks also check for pet hair and odours, which a thorough clean is far better placed to pass once animals are no longer in the property.

          

          

            How is an end of tenancy clean different from a regular deep clean?
            
A regular deep clean aims to make a home feel fresh and visibly clean. An end of tenancy clean is judged against a document — the inventory and check-out checklist. The team works room by room through that checklist, cleaning inside the oven, fridge and cupboards, removing limescale and dealing with marks and scuffs, because the standard is the property returned as found at move-in, not just looking clean on the surface. That is why it takes longer and is priced by property size rather than by the hour.

          

          

            Can mould and damp marks be dealt with in the clean?
            
Surface mould — the black spotting that builds up on bathroom sealant, tiles and kitchen grout — can usually be cleaned and treated as part of the end of tenancy clean. Deep or structural damp is different: penetrating or rising damp, or mould caused by a leak or a condensation problem, needs a specialist. It should be reported to your landlord or letting agent rather than painted over, which only hides the problem and can make a check-out dispute worse.

          

          

            What's different about an end of tenancy clean for a furnished flat versus an unfurnished one?
            
In an unfurnished flat the team cleans empty rooms, so the work is all surfaces, appliances and floors. In a furnished flat they also clean around and under the landlord's furniture — wiping down wardrobes, tables and shelving, and vacuuming upholstery — which adds time and typically £25–£40 to the quote. The inventory standard is the same either way: furnished items are checked item by item against the check-in report, so scuffs or stains on the landlord's furniture need flagging, not just the rooms around them.

          

          

            How far in advance should I book, and can I book for the same week?
            
Ideally request your quotes one to two weeks before your move-out date, especially if your tenancy ends at the end of the month, when London slots fill first. Same-week bookings are often possible — mid-week dates are usually easier to secure than Fridays and weekends — but availability depends on the firms covering your postcode. If your dates are tight, put them in the notes field on the quote form so firms only respond if they can actually make them.

          

          

            Are windows cleaned inside and outside as part of an end of tenancy clean?
            
Inside windows — glass, frames, sills and tracks — are included in every standard clean, because inventory clerks check them closely. Exterior window cleaning is an optional add-on (£40 flat) and depends on safe access, so upper floors without suitable access may only be cleaned from inside. Practical tip: tick the exterior windows add-on on the quote form and note your floor level, so the firm can confirm access before the day.

          

          

            How does key handover work — can the cleaners collect keys from the letting agent?
            
Yes — most firms can collect keys from your letting agent or landlord before the clean and return them afterwards, so you do not need to be at the property. Collection needs to be agreed when you book, as the agent will usually only release keys against the firm's ID and your written permission. Practical tip: put the agent's name, branch address and your permission in the notes field on the quote form so there is no delay on the day.

          

          

            Is cleaning behind and under large appliances like the fridge and washing machine included?
            
Yes, where an appliance can be moved safely — the team will pull out freestanding fridges and washing machines to clean the floor and wall area behind and underneath them, where dust and spills collect. Plumbed-in or very heavy appliances that cannot be moved without disconnecting pipes or wiring will be cleaned around and as far underneath as is safely reachable. Practical tip: empty and defrost the fridge in advance and clear a path beside each appliance, so the team can move it without risking damage.

          

          

            Are cupboards, drawers and the inside of the fridge and freezer included in an end of tenancy clean?
            
Yes. Emptied cupboards and drawers are wiped inside as part of the standard clean, and the inside of the fridge and freezer is cleaned too. The fridge and freezer need to be emptied and switched off in advance so the freezer has time to defrost before the team arrives.

          

          

            What do landlords and letting agents check at the check-out inspection?
            
They compare the property against the check-in inventory report, room by room, and take photos as evidence. The closest attention goes to the oven and hob, bathroom limescale, floors, wall marks and the windows — the items most likely to be flagged if they fall short of the check-in standard.

          

          

            Is limescale on taps and showerheads removed as part of the clean?
            
Yes, taps and showerheads are descaled as part of the bathroom clean. Very heavy build-up may need extra time, and damage from long-term neglect may need a specialist rather than a standard clean.

          

          

            Can I book an end-of-tenancy clean for a property I am moving INTO, not out of?
            
Yes — the same service is often booked as a move-in or pre-tenancy clean for the incoming tenant. It follows the same inventory checklist, but there is no check-out inspection to pass, so the focus is hygiene before your furniture arrives: inside the oven, fridge and cupboards, descaled bathrooms and clean floors throughout. Booking it for the day before you move in means you start in a property that is clean to a professional standard, and you keep the invoice as a record of the condition you received it in.

          

          

            Do cleaners work on weekends and evenings?
            
Yes — most firms offer Saturday and Sunday cleans because tenancies often end at the weekend, and some offer early-evening starts for weekday handovers. Weekend slots are the first to fill, especially at month-end, and a small number of firms charge a supplement for Sundays or evenings, so confirm the total price when you book. Putting your preferred date and time in the notes field on the quote form helps firms respond only if they can cover it.

          

          

            Are balconies, gardens and garages included in the clean?
            
A basic tidy is usually included for a balcony — sweeping and wiping down — and a balcony or terrace wash is available as a £25 add-on if it needs more. Gardens and garages are not part of the standard inside clean: grass cutting, garden clearance, jet washing and clearing out a garage are extra jobs that need to be quoted separately. Mention any outside space in the notes field on the quote form so the firm can price it honestly before the day.
