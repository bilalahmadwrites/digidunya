Before you apply — document checklist

Keep these scanned and ready once, and every application after that takes minutes.

  • Passport-size photoWhite background, made to the portal's exact spec
  • CNIC / national ID — front & backBoth sides, corners visible, no flash glare
  • Updated CV / résuméOne or two pages, PDF format, your name in the file name
  • Degrees & transcriptsLatest qualification first, each page straight and readable
  • Experience / service certificatesFrom every previous employer you list on the form
  • Domicile & PRCWhere the advertisement asks for them (common in government jobs)
