Fraud se bachein: Koi bhi sarkari scheme paisay nahi mangti. Sirf 8171 number se aane wala SMS hi BISP ka asal SMS hai. Apna CNIC, PIN ya OTP kisi ko na dein, aur kisi agent ko paisay na dein — registration aur survey bilkul free hai.

BISP 8171 Status Check — 2 Asal Tareeqay

8171 ka matlab hai BISP ka official check system. Apni eligibility aur payment sirf in do sarkari tareeqon se check karein — koi website aap se CNIC mang kar “andar se” check nahi kar sakti.

1

SMS tareeqa (sab se asaan)

Apne mobile se apna 13 rakmi CNIC number (baghair dash ke) likh kar 8171 par SMS bhej dein. Thori dair me jawab aa jayega ke aap eligible hain ya nahi aur payment ayi hai ya nahi.

2

Website tareeqa

Browser me 8171.bisp.gov.pk kholein, apna CNIC aur tasveer me diya gaya code likhein, aur Search dabayein. Yahi aik hi official portal hai — isi jaisi doosri websites nakli ho sakti hain.

3

Register nahi hain?

Naya registration online nahi hota. Apne qareebi BISP Tehsil Office jayein aur NSER survey free me karwayein. Yahi survey aapka PMT score banata hai, jis se eligibility tay hoti hai.

Ye tool sirf number ki ginti aur format check karta hai — aapka CNIC kahin save ya send nahi hota, aur ye BISP ka record check nahi karta. Asal status sirf 8171 SMS ya official portal se milega. BISP helpline (free): 0800-26477.

Aam Sawalat

Wo sawal jo log sab se zyada poochte hain.

BISP me naam kese aata hai? Koi form bharna hai?
Koi online form nahi hai. BISP Tehsil Office me NSER survey hota hai — ghar ki aamdani, afrad aur asaasay pooche jate hain, aur us se PMT score banta hai. Kafalat ke liye score aam tor par 32 ya us se kam hona chahiye (ghar me mazoor fard ho to 37 tak). Survey bilkul free hai — koi paisay mange to wo fraud hai.
8171 par SMS bheja, jawab “eligible nahi” aaya. Ab kya karun?
Pehle ye confirm karein ke aapka NSER survey ho chuka hai aur record purana to nahi — har ghar ka survey waqt ke saath dobara hota hai. Qareebi BISP Tehsil Office ja kar apna record check aur update karwayein. Kisi agent ko paisay de kar score “kam” nahi karwaya ja sakta — jo ye dawa kare, wo jhoot bol raha hai.
Kya mard BISP Kafalat le sakta hai?
Nahi. Kafalat ki rakam ghar ki aurat ke naam par milti hai — shadi-shuda aurat jiska apna durust CNIC ho. Mardon ke liye oopar di gayi doosri schemes hain: PM Youth Loan, Kissan Card, Himmat Card waghera.
PM Youth Loan ke liye bank account ya guarantee chahiye?
Apply online hota hai PM Youth Programme ke portal par, aur loan partner banks dete hain. Sab se chhota tier — Rs 5 lakh tak, 0% markup — ke liye aam tor par bari guarantee nahi mangi jati; baray tiers ke liye bank apni sharaait rakhta hai. Umar 21–45 saal hai; IT / e-commerce karobar ke liye 18 saal se apply ho sakta hai.
Punjab ki scheme aur Federal (wafaqi) scheme me kya farq hai?
Federal schemes (BISP, PM Youth Loan, PM Laptop) pure Pakistan ke liye hain. Punjab schemes (Apni Chhat Apna Ghar, Himmat Card, Kissan Card, Honhaar) sirf Punjab ke rehne walon ke liye hain — in ke liye Punjab ka domicile / record aur aksar PSER survey me naam hona zaroori hota hai.
Koi shakhs call karke CNIC aur OTP mange to kya karun?
Foran call band kar dein. Sarkari daftar kabhi phone par OTP ya PIN nahi mangte. Sirf 8171 se aane wala SMS asal hai — kisi mobile number se aane wala “BISP” SMS nakli hai. Shikayat ke liye BISP helpline 0800-26477 par call karein.

Zaroori note: Ye aik ghair-sarkari (unofficial) guide hai jo aam logon ki madad ke liye asaan zuban me banayi gayi hai — is ka kisi sarkari idaray se talluq nahi. Rakam, aakhri tareekh aur sharait badal sakti hain; koi bhi qadam uthane se pehle scheme ki official website ya helpline par lazmi confirm karein.

Maloomat check ki gayi: 13 November 2026 — BISP, PM Youth Programme aur Punjab Government ke elaanat ki bunyad par.
